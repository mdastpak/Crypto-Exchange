- The venue jobs of `cmd/market-worker` run apart from the market data ([#501](https://github.com/mdastpak/Crypto-Exchange/issues/501), [#514](https://github.com/mdastpak/Crypto-Exchange/issues/514)). The venue state snapshots and the routed order drivers have a runner of their own, which runs whenever a venue is enabled, also with `market_data.enabled: false`, is always live (each venue has its own mode), and is paused by a lockdown of `otc_routing` instead of `market_data`. See [`docs/venues.md#snapshots`](docs/venues.md#snapshots).
