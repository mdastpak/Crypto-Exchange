- Routed orders are pre-checked against the venue's book ([#515](https://github.com/mdastpak/Crypto-Exchange/issues/515)). An order whose limit the best ask or bid already passed is no longer placed only to rest until its deadline. It fails as `not_marketable`, and its quantity is routed elsewhere at once. Binance reads the public book ticker, and Nobitex reads `/market/stats`; both through the new optional `venues.BookReader`. The new gauges `otc_inventory_position`, `otc_inventory_limit`, and `otc_inventory_beyond_limit`, with an alert, show a position that a venue leg left to the inventory pushed past `otc.inventory_limits`.
