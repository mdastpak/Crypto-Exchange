- A leg of an OTC route at a venue now costs the withdrawal of what it buys back to our wallet when the trade has no network of its own, on the asset's `return_network` or the cheapest open network, and an optional `deposit_cost_bps` of the venue ([#510](https://github.com/mdastpak/Crypto-Exchange/issues/510)). A venue that cannot bring the currency back is not routed, and a route through a venue no longer looks cheaper than it is.
