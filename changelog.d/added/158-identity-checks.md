- The identity checks of users, which grant verification level 1 ([#158](https://github.com/mdastpak/Crypto-Exchange/issues/158)). See [Identity](docs/api.md#identity).
  - `POST /user/identity` checks a national ID and its date of birth at the national registry through the provider, and, when the account has a verified mobile number, that it is registered to the national ID. `GET /user/identity` shows the state: none, pending, verified, or rejected (mismatched or taken).
  - One person, one account: a verified national ID has a keyed blind index, unique, and a national ID verified for another account answers `409 national_id_taken`. Five failed checks a day at most (`429 identity_attempts_exceeded`).
  - A verified national ID with a card, IBAN, or account whose owner matched raises the user from level 0 to level 1, with the user's row locked first. A user with a verified identity may add a card or an IBAN at level 0 to get there.
  - The provider is checked with the date of birth too. `identity.bank_ownership.provider: jibit` checks cards, IBANs, and the national ID with Jibit's matching service, using the Jibit account of `identity.mobile_ownership`. The client follows Jibit's documented API, tested against a fake server, and must be checked against the contracted version before production.
  - When the provider cannot answer, an identity check stays pending, and a card or an IBAN is added pending and unusable (`202`, `ownership_pending`, `409 destination_ownership_pending` for a withdrawal). Each instance asks again every minute with a backoff to an hour, and nothing is approved on a failure; a mismatch removes the destination.
  - Migration 49: `identity_checks`, sealed from its first row; `user_national_ids` gains the blind index, the date of birth, encrypted, and a seal; `withdrawal_destinations` gains the pending state, sealed while it is set; `integrity_findings` accepts the two tables. National IDs, dates of birth, card numbers, and IBANs are never logged or put in errors.
