- The routed orders of the OTC trades ([#387](https://github.com/mdastpak/Crypto-Exchange/issues/387)), which place each venue leg of a trade's route at its venue and follow it to its end. See [`docs/otc-routing.md#routed-orders`](docs/otc-routing.md#routed-orders).
  - The order is planned in the transaction of the trade, with a reservation of the venue balance and the venue's daily usage, and is kept in the sealed `otc_routed_orders`, with each transition in the append-only `otc_routed_order_events` and on the outbox as `otc.routed_order_changed`.
  - Every order is a limit order within a slippage guard: the trade's price less the venue's fee, and `otc.routing.max_slippage_bps` of the reference price, on the venue's tick.
  - `cmd/market-worker` drives the orders of each enabled venue as `routed_orders:<venue>`; each order is held by one node at a time under a lease with a token, and is looked up by its derived client id before anything is placed again, with `otc.routing.unknown_grace_seconds` after a lost answer.
  - Each fill is posted to the ledger as `venue_fill` and moves the inventory's positions back. Past `otc.routing.order_timeout_seconds` an order is cancelled and its remainder routed again without the venues that failed it; with no route it is left to a review.
  - A lockdown of `otc_routing` stops the orders and refuses the trades that need one.
