- The watcher of the TRON deposits ([#68](https://github.com/mdastpak/Crypto-Exchange/issues/68)). See [deposits](docs/api.md#deposits).
  - Every block is read from a TronGrid node (`TRONGRID_API_KEY`): the TRX transfers and the TRC20 `Transfer` events of the listed tokens to the deposit addresses are recorded as pending, and credited once their block has 19 confirmations and is still in the chain. A transfer from the hot or the cold wallet is no deposit.
  - A block that does not follow the cursor is a reorg: the watcher reads again from `reorg_depth` blocks back, moves a deposit to its new block, and fails one that left the chain, which is pending again if it comes back. A block read twice credits nothing twice.
  - A confirmed deposit is held, still pending, when its currency's deposits are closed, the user's deposits are restricted, or the AML rules review or refuse it; one below the minimum fails. `POST /admin/deposits/{id}/credit` credits a held deposit after review (sensitive, audited), and `GET /admin/deposits` and `GET /user/deposits` list them.
  - Migration 55: a deposit names its network, transaction, event, and user, unique by network, transaction, and event, and is sealed with the cursor of each network: a deposit raised in SQL, or a cursor moved, is refused with its finding.
