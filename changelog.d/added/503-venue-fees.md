- The Binance connector reports the fees of its completed withdrawals ([#503](https://github.com/mdastpak/Crypto-Exchange/issues/503)). The reconciliation therefore posts each as `venue_fee` rather than holding the currency for a difference. The fees are read from a cursor kept as the `fees` snapshot (migration 48), from the last read less 3 days, and posted once by the id of the withdrawal. A dry-run venue reads them too. `venues.Fee` and `venues.FeeReporter` take the place of the types of `internal/venues/state`, and `Fees` takes the start of the window. See [`docs/venues.md#the-fees-of-the-venues`](docs/venues.md#the-fees-of-the-venues).
