- An admin review of the routed orders of the OTC trades ([#513](https://github.com/mdastpak/Crypto-Exchange/issues/513)). `GET /admin/otc/routed-orders` lists the orders that need a person, and why, with their transitions, their seal, and their unsealed deadlines. `POST /admin/otc/routed-orders/{id}/resolve` resolves one, with a step-up and an audit event: `handled` after a hedge by hand, or `retry` to route the remainder again. The new gauges `otc_routed_orders`, `otc_routed_order_oldest_seconds`, `otc_routed_order_unhedged`, `otc_routed_order_fill_seconds`, and `otc_routed_orders_unverified` come with alerts. Migration 57 adds the `resolved` status.
