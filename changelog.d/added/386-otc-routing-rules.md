- The routing rules of the OTC trades ([#386](https://github.com/mdastpak/Crypto-Exchange/issues/386)), which pick the exchange's own inventory, an external venue, or a split across venues for each trade. See [`docs/otc-routing.md`](docs/otc-routing.md).
  - Rules match a trade by market, side, network, notional, region, and verification level, and bound each venue by a share of its balance, a reserve, a daily notional, and a cost ceiling in basis points; `own_first` or `cheapest`, with weights to split a trade across the venues of one priority.
  - With `otc.routing.enabled`, a quote is priced with the cost of its route (`route_bps`) and refused with `otc_no_route` when no rule gives one; the execution plans the route again against fresh state and refuses with `quote_not_routable` when it is gone or dearer. The trade records the route.
  - Each change is a new sealed version in `otc_routing_rules`, audited as `admin.otc_routing_rules`, which every instance applies within `otc.routing.cache_seconds`. An international release refuses a rule that names an Iranian venue.
  - `GET` and `PUT /api/v1/admin/otc/routing/rules` (with a step-up) and `POST /api/v1/admin/otc/routing/explain`, which shows how the rules route a trade without placing anything.
  - `venues.exchanges.<name>.fee_bps` and `slippage_bps`, the cost of a leg at a venue, and the sealed daily usage of the venues in `otc_routing_usage`.
